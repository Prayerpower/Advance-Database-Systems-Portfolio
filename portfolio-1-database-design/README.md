Chosen Case Study
Dove Bank Ltd is a simulated commercial bank that provides everyday retail banking services to individual customers in Nigeria. For this portfolio, the bank serves three customers: James John, Ifeanyi Chime and Adejoke Samson.

The database manages the bank's customers, their bank accounts and the financial transactions made on those accounts. One customer can hold more than one account, for example a savings and a current account, and each account can have many transactions.

The users of the database are bank staff such as tellers and customer service officers, customers who view their own account details, and database administrators who maintain the database, its security and its backups.

The main data stored is customer details (name, email and phone number), account details (account number, account type and balance) and transaction records (transaction type, amount and date).

The database supports opening new accounts, checking account balances, recording deposits and withdrawals, transferring funds between accounts and viewing a customer's transaction history.
